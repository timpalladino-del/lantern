## Description
Most households already own a week of food, a case of water and a drawer of batteries. What they don't have is a list. Lantern keeps it.

KNOW WHAT YOU HAVE
Scan a barcode or a printed date and it's logged. Lantern knows the shelf life of ninety-plus staples, so a can without a date still gets one. Everything has a place: pantry, basement, car, go-bag.

USE IT BEFORE IT GOES TO WASTE
One quiet weekly note about what to eat, rotate or replace. Nothing nags. The point is to stop throwing good food away.

READINESS IN DAYS, FOR YOUR ACTUAL PEOPLE
Add who lives here, their ages and medications. Lantern turns the pantry into calories per person and the medicine cabinet into days of supply. Pets count too.

A MEDICATION CARD TO HAND TO ANYONE
Dose, times, take with food, don't drive. One card for the whole house to share as text or print for the fridge and the go-bag. Family members can fill in their own profile from a link.

GO-BAGS AND KITS
Start a go-bag, car kit or pet bag from a ready-made list and pack what you already own. Lantern shows what's still missing.

THE TOOLKIT, FREE FOR EVERYONE, ALWAYS
• Outage mode: fridge and freezer clocks, generator and carbon-monoxide cards, battery tips, a check-in reminder
• SOS strobe and flashlight, screen light and candle
• Alarm whistle and sleep sounds
• Emergency numbers, your household meeting plan, shutoff photos
• Know-how cards from CDC, USDA, FEMA, Red Cross and the National Weather Service
• Compass, Wireless Emergency Alert settings, NOAA Weather Radio frequencies

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No cloud. No analytics. Everything stays on your iPhone. The only thing Lantern ever sends anywhere is a barcode's digits to a public food database, and you can turn that off.

FREE UNTIL YOU NEED IT
Lantern is free for one person and fifty items, with the entire Toolkit included. Duo covers two people for a small yearly price. Family covers up to six people (or ten). Every plan is shared with your Apple Family Sharing group and starts with a 7-day free trial. Nothing in the Toolkit is ever behind the paywall.

Lantern gives general information from public sources for planning. It is not medical or safety advice. In an emergency, call 911 first.

## Review notes
Local-first app with no account. To see the paywall, add a second person under Settings → People, pets and medications, or add a 51st item. The Toolkit tab is fully usable without a purchase. The Outage mode check-in and medication reminders are local notifications. Camera is used on-device for barcode and date scanning. Location permission is used only for the compass heading and is never stored.
