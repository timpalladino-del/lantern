# Lantern brand direction (v1, November 2026)

**Tagline:** Ready for when you need it most.
**Supporting line:** Know what you have.
**App Store name:** Lantern: Home Supplies · **Subtitle:** Peace of mind, on the shelf (or: Supplies, dates & gear)

The idea: the thing you reach for when the power dies. Warm light, not a bunker. The app is about looking
after the people you love when something goes wrong. No doomsday, no panic, no tactical language.

## Color
Dark mode is the home theme. Light mode is a cool dawn grey, not cream.

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Night | #141B2B | Dark background |
| Slate | #1C2436 | Dark cards and rows |
| Glow | #F5B041 | The accent: icon, glow, progress bars, active tab. Never buttons. |
| Paper | #F2F4F7 | Text in dark mode, primary button in dark mode |
| Glow deep | #D98E1F | Amber for light mode fills |
| Dawn | #F1F3F6 | Light background |
| Ink | #1A2232 | Text in light mode, primary button in light mode, text on amber |
| Mist | #9AA5B5 | Secondary text, dividers |
| Moss | #5FA37A / #3F7D57 | Fine, checked, covered |
| Ember | #E07A4C / #C4602F | Use soon, past best-by |
| Signal | #E0534E / #B93A36 | Medicine expired, CO safety only |

Amber is for light, never for buttons and never for body text. Buttons are Ink on light, Paper on dark, so a tap target never looks like a warning.

## Type
Marketing: Bricolage Grotesque (display), Nunito Sans (body), IBM Plex Mono (dates, barcodes).
In the app: SF Pro, SF Pro Rounded for section titles, SF Mono for dates and barcodes, tabular figures.

## Icon
Chosen 2026-11-03. A solid navy hurricane-lantern silhouette cut out of a flat amber disc of light, with a
darker amber halo ring, on navy. Generated in Runway, then snapped to the brand palette: Night #141B2B,
Glow #F5B041, halo #C98A24. Dark appearance uses a deeper ground (#0B1019); the Tinted variant is grayscale
on black. Source render, final 1024 and a size preview live in `docs/brand/icon/`. Clear-mode glass layers
(Icon Composer) still to do on the Mac.

## Voice
Say: ready, stocked, supplies, use first, a bad week, the outage, covered for 9 days, checked, still fine.
Never: prepper, survival, SHTF, bug out, tactical, loadout, threat, doomsday, apocalypse, mission, countdown, score.

## Don'ts
Camouflage, stencil fonts, flames, skulls, hazard stripes, countdown timers, red badges, percentage readiness
scores, flashlights or camping lanterns as the icon, stock storm photos.
