BEFORE RELEASE, THE SHORT VERSION
------------------------------------------------------------------

  Things you can do while it is in review:

  1. Decide about the paywall screenshot (punch-list.md, item 1).
  2. App Store Connect pre-flight: Paid Apps agreement Active,
     W-9 on file, Small Business Program enrolled (85 percent
     instead of 70; it is not retroactive).
  3. Request Family offer codes: Subscriptions, Family 6, Offer
     Codes, one-time use, free, 1 year, 100 codes.
  4. Build the Reddit Ads campaign and leave it Scheduled for Day 15.
  5. Join the subreddits and leave five helpful comments with no
     mention of the app.
  6. Put the live App Store badge on the site, ready to swap in.

  Things you cannot do until the app is live:

  - Apple Ads. Build both campaigns on release morning.
  - Campaign links in App Analytics. Day 2 or 3. Use the direct
    link on Day 1; attribution costs nothing on a day when only
    one thing is running.

Full detail is in the TONIGHT tab. About 60 minutes.

------------------------------------------------------------------
STANDING RULES
------------------------------------------------------------------

  No em dashes. No price, "free" or "buy" in a headline or ad.
  No employer, no job. Never say prepper, survival, bug out,
  tactical, threat or doomsday in anything public. The App Store
  link is always the direct link, never "search for it".
  Nine helpful Reddit contributions for every one app mention.
