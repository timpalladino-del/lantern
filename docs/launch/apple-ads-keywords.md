# Apple Ads: campaigns, keywords, bids

Apple Ads cannot see an app that is not live. Build both campaigns on release morning, right after the app
is confirmed live and the trial test passes. ads.apple.com, the developer Apple ID, the Advanced experience
(Basic hides the controls below). Country: United States. Devices: iPhone. Search Match: OFF on both
(Castlewise lesson: on a small budget it brings the wrong people; harvest terms from a separate Discovery
campaign on Day 16 instead).

Apple charges what it takes to win the auction, not your max bid. A brand-new app has near-zero relevance,
so a low bid loses every auction and shows nothing for days. Start at the ceilings below and read the
dashboard, which lags a day.

## The three mechanical rules

1. Pause any keyword over $2.50 per install once it has 30 taps.
2. Raise the bid by $0.10 on any keyword under $1.50 per install.
3. Never raise the daily budget to chase a good day. The cap only moves at the Day 22 reserve decision.

Keyword bids override the ad group default. Raising the ad group bid does nothing until each keyword's own
bid is raised too.

## Day 10 · Custom product page

App Store Connect, Custom Product Pages, new page named "Outage first". Screenshots reordered so Outage
mode is first and the medication card second. Promotional text: "When the power goes, start the clocks.
Fridge, freezer, generator, and the medication card you can hand to anyone." Submit it, then point ad
group A at it.

## Day 16 · Discovery

New campaign, $2 a day, no keywords, Search Match ON. Every search term in it that produces an install gets
added to Exact Match Core at the next readout, then added as a negative in Discovery so you do not pay
twice. Dismiss the Recommendations tab outright; it produces generic high-volume words ("home", "safety
app") that spend the budget on nothing.

## What to expect

On Castlewise, exact match showed almost nothing for the first two weeks even at $2.50: a new app with two
ratings and a thin keyword field does not get entered into those auctions. Apple Ads is a thermometer for
how the store already ranks you, not a way to get ranked. The fix is ratings and a stronger keyword field on
1.0.1, not a higher bid. Budget accordingly: $200 over the month, and move anything that has not produced an
install by Day 22 to Reddit.
