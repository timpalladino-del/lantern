# Lantern Facebook Page: content plan, weeks 1 to 4

Rules: no price, no employer, no em dashes, iPhone only, no prepper or survival language. Links never in
the post body; the App Store link goes in the first comment with the fb-post campaign token once App
Analytics has issued it: `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6830418364?pt=129222714&ct=fb-post&mt=8`
(confirm the pt value in App Analytics, Campaigns). Until then, the direct link:
`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6830418364`.

## What the page is for

The audience is households, 30 to 65, the people who fill the tub before a storm and ask in a
neighbourhood group afterward. Every post is a household problem first and the app second. CastleWise's
best-performing pattern: problem in the first line, one real tip the reader can use without the app, one
line on what the app adds, "Link in the comments". Three hashtags at most.

## Cadence

Four posts a week for four weeks, then read Insights and keep what got reach.

- Mon: a household problem, as a photo or a short vertical video of the app solving it.
- Wed: one app feature, as a screenshot or a screen recording.
- Fri: a seasonal tip, text and one image.
- Sat: the store screenshot of the week, or a plain-text tip.

Best times for this audience: 7 to 9 PM Eastern on weekdays, 10 AM Saturday.

## After week 4

Read Insights (reach, 3-second views, link clicks on the first comment) and the fb-post campaign in App
Store Connect. Keep the formats that got reach, drop the rest. Seasonal posts (hurricane season end,
winter storms, wildfire smoke) go out the week the weather turns, with the local tags from the Nov 3 post
pattern.

  link kismetstudio.io/lantern/privacy/, founding date = release date.
- Cover: social/lantern-facebook-cover-1640x924.png. Words stay in the desktop-safe band; the lower left

- Three links: site, App Store, press page.
- Scheduled posts cannot take a comment until they go live, so schedule with the link in the body and
  move it to a comment once the post is up, or post by hand at the times above.
