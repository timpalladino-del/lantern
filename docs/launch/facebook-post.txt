THE POST
------------------------------------------------------------------

So this is a thing I made.

It is called Lantern, and it went live on the App Store this
morning. It is for the food, water, medicine and gear you already
keep for a bad week.

The idea came from standing in the dark with a flashlight, knowing
there was a week of food somewhere in the house and not being able
to say where, or how old any of it was. Most households are more
ready than they think. What they do not have is a list. Lantern
keeps it.

Scan a can and it knows when to use it. Tell it who lives in your
house and it tells you how many days you are covered for, in real
calories and real gallons, not a score. Every person has a
medication card you can hand to a neighbour, a nurse or a shelter
desk. When the power goes, Outage mode starts the clocks on the
fridge and the freezer.

And the emergency toolkit is free for everyone, always: the SOS
strobe, the whistle, the fridge clock, the medication card, the
know-how cards from the CDC and FEMA. No account, no cloud.
Everything stays on your phone, which still works when nothing
else does.

I would love your help with two small things:

1. Download it. Even if you never open it again, early downloads
   matter more than I can explain.
2. If you do try it and it helps, leave a rating. Honest ones. The
   first handful of ratings decide whether strangers ever trust it.

Link in the comments. And if you find something broken, tell me. I
would much rather hear it from you than read it in a review.
