## Lantern, a calm household readiness app for iPhone, is on the App Store

*Scan the pantry, see how many days your family is covered, hand anyone a medication card. The emergency
toolkit is free for everyone, and nothing leaves the phone.*

**[CITY], Florida, [DATE]** Kismet Studio today released Lantern: Household Readiness, an iPhone app for
the food, water, medicine and gear that households already keep for a bad week, and for the people who
depend on them.

Most American households own more than they realise: a week of food, a case of water, a drawer of
batteries, a medicine cabinet. What they do not have is a list. Lantern keeps it. Scan a barcode or a
printed date and the item is logged; a built-in shelf-life library, hand-built from USDA, CDC and FDA
sources, gives a date to the cans that never had one. Add who lives in the house, their ages, sizes,
medications and pets, and Lantern turns the pantry into days of food and water for those actual people.
Not a score. Real calories and real gallons against the Red Cross and CDC targets.

"The idea came from standing in the dark with a flashlight, knowing there was a week of food in the house
and not being able to say where," said Tim Palladino, who built Lantern. "The last ten years have put most
households through at least one long outage. Being ready for the next one should not feel like joining a
movement. It should feel like a tidy pantry."

**What Lantern does**

- **Know what you have.** Barcode and printed-date scanning on the device, a shelf-life library for more
  than ninety staples with a source on every entry, and nutrition facts pulled in from the barcode.
- **Readiness in days, for your actual people.** Calories per person follow Mifflin-St Jeor when weight
  and height are known, otherwise the Dietary Guidelines bands. Water follows the CDC gallon-a-day. Pets
  count too.
- **A medication card to hand to anyone.** Dose, times, take with food, who prescribed it, the pharmacy
  number. One card for the whole house, shared as text or printed for the fridge and the go-bag.
- **Profiles that travel in a link.** Family members fill in their own details from an invitation and send
  them back. No account, no server; the link is the transport.
- **Outage mode.** When the power goes, start the clocks on the fridge and freezer, open the generator and
  carbon monoxide cards, set a check-in reminder for the person who looks after you.
- **The toolkit, free for everyone, always.** SOS strobe and flashlight, alarm whistle, compass, the NOAA
  Weather Radio frequencies, emergency numbers, a household meeting plan, and know-how cards from the CDC,
  USDA, FEMA, the Red Cross and the National Weather Service. No purchase, no sign-up, works with no
  signal.
- **Private by design.** No account, no cloud, no analytics. The only thing Lantern ever sends anywhere
  is a barcode's digits to a public food database, and that can be turned off.

**Availability**

Lantern: Household Readiness is available now on the App Store for iPhone running iOS 18 or later. The
toolkit and tracking for one person are free. Family plans cover a whole household for a yearly price,
shared through Apple Family Sharing, with a seven-day free trial.
