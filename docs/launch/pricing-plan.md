# Pricing plan

## What is live at launch

| Plan | Covers | Price | Where it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 1 person, 50 items, every kit limit of 1, the whole Toolkit | $0 | default |
| Family 6 | up to 6 people, unlimited items and kits, everyone's medications, profile links, equipment reminders | $12.99 a year, 7-day free trial | paywall, default selection |
| Family 10 | the same for up to 10 people | $19.99 a year, 7-day free trial | paywall |
| Group 50 | up to 50 people | $49.99 a year | App Store Connect only, hidden in the app |

Family Sharing is on for both Family plans, so one subscription covers everyone in the Apple family.
Product IDs: io.kismetstudio.lantern.family6, .family10, .group50. Subscription group 22542888.

The paywall appears at the second person and the 51st item, never during Outage mode and never on
anything in the Toolkit. That rule is in the code and in the store copy. Keep it.

## Where money is and is not mentioned

- App Store listing: "Free until you need it" and the plan names, no numbers in the description. Prices
  are on the paywall and in App Store Connect where Apple shows them.
- Screenshots: no price anywhere. See punch-list.md item 1.
- kismetstudio.io/lantern/: the plans section shows $12.99 and $19.99. This is a conscious exception to
  the old Kismet "no money on the site" rule, made for Lantern because the free tier is the pitch and a
  visitor who cannot find the price assumes the worst. Keep the numbers accurate or remove them; never
  stale.
- Facebook, Reddit, Instagram, ads: never a number, never "free" as a headline, never "buy". "The toolkit
  is free for everyone" is a feature statement and is fine in body copy.

## Month one: no discounts, no changes

CastleWise lesson: one purchase in 22 downloads is not a rate. Nothing about price gets decided until there
are 100 downloads and 30 days. Specifically:

- No launch discount. The trial is the discount.
- No "launch pricing ends" promotional text. There is no launch price to end.
- The number that decides anything later is **revenue per download**, not conversion. At $12.99 and a 5
  percent trial-to-paid rate, that is about 55 cents net per download after Apple's 15 percent. A $9.99
  plan would need 6.5 percent to match. Do not reason from conversion alone.

## What to watch (Tracker tab)

- Trial starts per 100 downloads (App Store Connect, Subscriptions, Trends).
- Trial-to-paid after the first cohort's seven days clear (Day 8 onward).
- Where people hit the paywall. The app does not report this (no analytics, by design), so it comes from
  reviews and from testers. If people hit it at the second person and bounce, the moment is wrong, not the
  price.

## Day 30 decisions, in order

1. **Trial starts under 3 per 100 downloads:** the paywall copy or the moment is wrong. Fix the screen
   before touching the price.
2. **Trial starts fine, trial-to-paid under 25 percent:** the seven days are not showing the value.
   Candidates: a day-5 local reminder that says what the household is covered for; the medication card
   share in the trial's first session. Not a price cut.
3. **Both fine:** leave it alone for another month. Then consider a monthly option ($1.99) for people
   who want it for hurricane season only, which is a real pattern in Florida.
4. **Group 50:** surfaces in the app only when someone asks for it in writing. The first church or
   neighbourhood group that asks gets it switched on in their build the same week.

## Offer codes (the only "free" there is)

- Beta testers: one year of Family 6 each. Nine codes.
- r/AppHookup giveaway: 25 one-year codes, Day 16.
- Press and reviewers: on request, logged.
- Everything else: no. Codes are a thank-you, not a channel.

Create them in App Store Connect, Subscriptions, Family 6, Offer Codes: one-time use, Free, 1 year. Set an
expiry a month out. Log every code: who, when sent, when redeemed.
