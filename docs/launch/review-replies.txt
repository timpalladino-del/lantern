REVIEW REPLIES AND THE SECOND RATING ASK
========================================

Developer replies show publicly under the review. Keep them short,
specific, no sales pitch, no em dashes. Paste in App Store Connect,
Ratings and Reviews, Reply. Reply to every written review within a
day; a reply is the one thing a stranger reads after the review.

--- Five stars, general ---

Thank you. The weekly "use this first" note is the part I am proudest
of, so I am glad it landed. If there is something you wish it tracked
and it does not, the feedback link in Settings goes straight to me.

--- Five stars, mentions the medication card ---

Thank you. The card exists because the night we needed it, the list
was in my head and nowhere else. If a nurse or a neighbour ever reads
yours, I would like to hear how it held up.

--- Five stars, mentions the free toolkit ---

Thank you for saying so. The toolkit stays free on every plan, always,
and nothing in it will ever ask you to pay during an outage. That is a
promise, not a launch offer.

--- Four stars, wants a feature ---

Thank you, and noted: [FEATURE] is on the list. The next update
already has [NEXT THING]; if [FEATURE] is small it may ride along.
If you want to be told when it ships, the feedback link in Settings
reaches me directly.

--- Three stars, confused by the paywall ---

Fair, and thank you for saying it plainly. One person and fifty items
are free, and the whole toolkit is free for everyone. Duo and Family
are only for a house with more than one person's medications and
supplies to track. If the message in the app did not make that clear,
that is on me and I will reword it.

--- Two or one stars, a bug ---

I am sorry. That should not happen and I would like to fix it this
week. Could you send a line to team@kismetstudio.io with your iPhone
model and what you tapped just before it went wrong? I read every
message myself.

--- Two or one stars, "I expected X" ---

Thank you for trying it. Lantern is deliberately the calm version:
a list of what you have and how long it lasts, not threat levels or
checklists for every scenario. If that is not what you were after,
I understand, and I am sorry it cost you the time. If there is one
thing that would have made it worth keeping, I would genuinely like
to know.

--- Any review that mentions a crash ---

Thank you for reporting it, and sorry. If you can, open Settings,
Privacy and Security, Analytics, Analytics Data on your iPhone and
send any file starting with "Lantern" to team@kismetstudio.io. That
file is what I need to find it.

ratings.

That is the whole ask. Thank you to everyone who already did.
