phone. The interesting problems were the shelf-life library (every entry cites USDA, CDC or FDA), the
calorie math (Mifflin-St Jeor when it knows you, Dietary Guidelines bands when it does not), and family

## Reply when someone asks "what makes it different from a checklist app"

It is not a checklist. It is a count. A checklist tells you to have water; Lantern tells you that you have
six days of it for the four people in your house, and that the real limit is Maria's blood pressure
medication, which runs out in nine.

## Reply when someone says "I'm not a prepper"

Neither are we. Lantern is for the tidy version: knowing what is in the pantry, rotating the water, keeping
the medication list where someone else can read it. If you have ever thrown out a can because you were not
sure how old it was, it is for you.
