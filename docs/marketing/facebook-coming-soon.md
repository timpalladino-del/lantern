To everyone on the Space Coast, around Lake Okeechobee and down in South Florida tonight: we're thinking of you. A foot of rain in a day is not something anyone plans for. If you need a shelter, your county emergency management page has the list, and 211 can point you to help.

If you're dry but the lights are flickering: fill the tub, charge everything, and move the freezer food you'd hate to lose to the top shelf.

This week is exactly why we've been building Lantern.

Lantern is a calm little app for the food, water, medicine and gear you keep for a bad week. Scan a can and it knows when to use it. Tell it who lives in your house and it tells you how many days you're covered. Add your generator or your alarms and it reminds you to check them. No account, no cloud, no subscription. Everything stays on your phone, which still works when nothing else does.

Coming soon to iPhone.

Ready for when you need it most.
